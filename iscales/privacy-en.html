iScales on your device

iScales does not require a user account. Settings, your own patterns and practice data are stored locally on your device. These data are not transmitted to a server operated by Klaus Storck. The app does not use analytics or tracking services.

When you use the microphone feature, iScales asks for your permission. The audio signal is processed on your device for pitch detection and is not transmitted to us. You can revoke microphone access in iOS Settings. Piano samples are bundled with the iOS app.

You can delete patterns in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data; this does not delete device backups or files you have exported yourself. When exporting or sharing files, you choose the receiving app or person.

Pro purchases through Apple

The one-time Pro purchase and purchase restoration use Apple and StoreKit. The app receives product information and entitlement status to unlock Pro features. We do not receive payment details or login credentials for your Apple Account. Apple's processing is covered by Apple's privacy information.
